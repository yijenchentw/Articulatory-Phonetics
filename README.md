This is an online exercise for a review of the articulation of English consonants and vowels.

The website was designed for Taiwanese students taking Articulatory Phonetics, an university-level EMI course.

All answers are aligned with A Course in Phonetics (6th ed.) by Peter Ladefoged & Keith Johnson.
